## Player behaviour

Find and recruit the Foreman through ordinary nearby interaction (E or the Discoveries button). The reward is permanent and immediately usable. Ctrl+C enters area selection; drag around complete nearby machines. A successful copy previews the saved clipboard automatically; Ctrl+V retrieves it later. Click a destination’s upper-left tile to stamp the entire layout. R rotates clockwise, H mirrors horizontally and V mirrors vertically while using the clipboard. Buttons provide the same actions. Escape/right-click cancels the preview without deleting the saved layout. Copy/paste and button actions return focus and scroll to the canvas on the compact stacked layout; native text inputs keep their usual keyboard handling.

The visible clipboard includes a layout thumbnail with directions, machine counts and raw material price. A carried packed machine replaces its own raw material cost. A stamp is **atomic**: all placements and settings succeed together at normal cost, or no machine is placed and no stock is spent. Occupancy, terrain, unlocks, reach and shortages remain real. A successful stamp is one construction-history group; Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y reuse current packing, capacity and refund rules. Undo does not rewind production or recover spent ammunition.

| History | Existing 50-group limit, current reach/packing eligibility, conserved returned contents and current settings protection remain. A busy assembler, fractional contents or unavailable capacity can still refuse packing. |
| Scope boundary | Saved folders/icons/import-export, ghost orders, autonomous Line truck work and area removal remain P8-02–04. This increment neither changes the truck nor grants free repairs/base recovery. |

## Save and replay compatibility

Real EX-08C fresh/network/Turbine checkpoints migrate without changing earlier recruits, Turbine location/state or any existing machines. They add only an unrecruited Foreman and the new metadata version; no clipboard or capability is silently granted. Earlier version-1/3/5/6/7 migration tests retain their old inputs and now assert metadata 10. The prior guide test compares its original four recruits separately from the new fifth recruit.

A new shelter reservation can intersect a historical building command. Older browser saves therefore retain their state and logs but mark fresh-campaign replay provenance incomplete; this does not discard their progress or prevent continued play. New P8-01 saves with complete logs preserve replay through clipboard operations. Current saves reject malformed clipboards, missing/duplicated recruits and invalid settings. Current simulation save/load continuation remains deterministic.
