# Early-reception complaints in the factory genre — standards audit (2026-09-04)

Method: Steam top-rated negative reviews, Steam forum searches, VaporLens review aggregates, press. Reddit was unreachable (403 via both fetch and the Reddit MCP); RPS/Eurogamer blocked; the web-search budget expired after ~25 queries and Steam's forum-search endpoint rate-limited the last pass. Gaps are marked **thin** or **not found**. All dates are as shown on the source.

## Evidence per candidate complaint

**1. No undo/redo.** Loud and persistent, and it appears at launch. DSP, within four weeks of EA (Jan 2021): "DEMOLITION MODE SERIOUSLY NEEDS AN 'UNDO' BUTTON" (30 Jan 2021), "UNDO please!" (21 Feb 2021), and the 92-reply QoL thread: "Undo. I accidentally delete stuff all the time" ([search](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/discussions/search/?q=undo&gidforum=0&include_deleted=0), [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/discussions/0/3108016336243113553/)). Captain of Industry: eight separate undo threads 2022–2026 ("Ctrl + X is sad please fix" Jul 2022; "Undo/Redo Button" May 2023; "I find the lack of an undo feature to be infuriating, especially this late in development") ([search](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1594320/discussions/search/?q=undo&gidforum=0&include_deleted=0)). Satisfactory: "No undo after dismantling 50 things (including 1 you didn't want to dismantle)" and "'An undo button' / My kingdom for a horse" ([1](https://steamcommunity.com/app/526870/discussions/0/3283695222735846476/), [2](https://steamcommunity.com/app/526870/discussions/0/3830914462349576057/)); CTRL+Z not working for blueprints is a recurring gripe ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/526870/discussions/0/4843148768107649944/)). Techtonica: "Mass Dismantle" (Aug 2023) and "UNDO function for the M.O.L.E" (Jun 2024) ([search](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/search/?q=undo&gidforum=0&include_deleted=0)). FOUNDRY suggestions: "MOST IMPORTANT: have a button/combo to undo the last action" ([search](https://steamcommunity.com/app/983870/discussions/search/?q=undo&gidforum=0&include_deleted=0)). shapez 2 shipped undo at EA and it is discussed as a positive mechanic ("undo refunds the cost") ([search](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2162800/discussions/search/?q=undo&gidforum=0&include_deleted=0)). Loudness: forum-heavy, review-light — it surfaces in top negative reviews only via "accidental deletion" (item 5).

**2. Belts one segment at a time / no drag / no auto-underground.** Press notices it on day one. PC Gamer on FOUNDRY (Wes Fenlon, 1 May 2024): connecting belts is "tedious and fiddly … I don't like having to manually stick a curved piece on a conveyor run instead of being able to freeform drag it" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sim/foundry-is-a-very-calm-pleasant-building-game-id-play-if-i-got-tired-of-several-other-ones/)). bit-tech on DSP (15 Feb 2021): belt building an "infuriatingly slow process" because drones place each piece ([bit-tech](https://bit-tech.net/reviews/gaming/pc/dyson-sphere-program-review/1/)). Astro Colony top negative review: "you have to stop placing belts, select a certain tool" to orient them ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1614550/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). Mindustry review: "could only drag straight lines and had to let go and grab again" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1127400/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). Techtonica EA impressions: belt placement "really fiddly when you're working with tight spaces" ([GamerMatters](https://gamermatters.com/techtonica-early-access-impressions/)); Techtonica did ship drag-belts with auto-arch ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/0/3812910932504728413/)). Loudness: medium — press and forums, rarely the headline of a negative review.

**3. No blueprints / copy-paste (or gated).** The loudest single QoL absence in the dataset. FOUNDRY: VaporLens counts "Lack of copy/paste and blueprint features" at 18 mentions, "considered essential" ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/983870/foundry)); the 36-hour "isn't respecting my time" post lists "Missing blueprint system" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/983870/discussions/0/4361250551850063518/)); Paradox's FAQ still lists blueprints as "In Production" ([FAQ](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/foundry/faq)). Satisfactory's top negative reviews, 2020–2024: "No ability to copy/paste or mass-build or create blueprints" (Nov 2021), "constrained by 5x5x8 block limit" (Oct 2024) ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/526870/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). DSP launched without them (Jan 2021): "no blueprints and multiplayer in 2021? really…" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/discussions/0/3106889614406421907/?ctp=5)); 445-hour negative review (Mar 2021) names "Blueprints and component delivery via drones" as the missing feature ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)); Wikipedia confirms they arrived later in 2021 ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyson_Sphere_Program)). Gating behind research also draws fire: CoI "I don't want to wait half an hour to discover blueprints" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1594320/discussions/0/573794767997055154/)), "200 CP1 and 200 CP2. Ouch!" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1594320/discussions/0/3785876382868797825/)). Techtonica (HardcoreGamer, Nov 2024): "blueprints are desperately needed" ([review](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-techtonica/)). Factory Town: "no blueprint system to help speed things along" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/860890/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). 2025 release ShapeHero Factory: "no saved blueprints. No selection, copy and paste" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2389040/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated)).

**4. No production statistics / congestion display.** Desynced top negative review (99.9 h): "It is crucial in a factory automation game to tell people the rate at which materials are consumed or produced" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1450900/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). FOUNDRY 85.9-h review: "Missing production statistics/metrics" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/983870/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)); FOUNDRY suggestion thread asks for "machine uptime statistics" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/983870/discussions/0/4343240481095229278/)). Astro Colony: "No belt speed statistics visible" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1614550/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). Techtonica: metrics only display "when the device is active" ([HardcoreGamer](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-techtonica/)). CoI has ten stats threads asking for *better* stats ("Production Statistics? Where?", May 2025) ([search](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1594320/discussions/search/?q=statistics&gidforum=0&include_deleted=0)). Satisfactory 1.1 added belt "throughput monitors" six years after EA ([Yahoo/PC Gamer](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/satisfactorys-biggest-patch-since-launch-110000583.html)). Loudness: medium; rarely the lead complaint.

**5. Accidental deletion, no refund / no confirmation.** DSP, 15 Feb 2021, 65 replies: "SERIOUSLY! Ask before deleting what we can't hold!" — "damned irritating … you click on it and didn't realize that you were still in delete mode"; "I accidentally deleted twice in the few hours of playing" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/discussions/0/4361143473111338163/)). Astro Colony review: "Control right click will delete any build able item without confirmation" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1614550/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). FOUNDRY review (Nov 2024): "Hold down the button too long and you delete something else" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/983870/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). Desynced: "having to manually pick up every item when destroying a building" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1450900/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). Satisfactory's dismantle timer is defended precisely as the safeguard: "saves your players way more headache"; "Dismantle returns all materials (thankfully)" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/526870/discussions/0/3830914462349576057/)).

**6. Cannot drop/clear the cursor or hand.** **Not found** in any review, forum thread or press piece examined. (Closest: FOUNDRY hand-craft queue vanishing — "the belts start accumulating in my inventory, and then... they go away" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/983870/discussions/0/4343240481095229278/)) — which is an inventory bug, not a cursor complaint.)

**7. QoL that exists but is hidden and never taught.** Factory Town reviews: elevated belts need Page Up/Down, which "really needs to be told in game"; paths have "vertical effectiveness limits not mentioned in-game" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/860890/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). DSP review: "must keep dsp-wiki.com open at all times" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). Desynced: "No warnings, no errors, no documentation, just trial and error" ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1450900/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)). CoI: 44 "steep learning curve" mentions, "requires external guides to grasp basic mechanics" ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/1594320/captain_of_industry)). Techtonica: "every item uses the V key to switch modes except for the mole, which wants Z" ([HardcoreGamer](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-techtonica/)). Factorio itself hides blueprint buttons but shortcuts "function regardless of button visibility" ([forums](https://forums.factorio.com/viewtopic.php?t=95873)). Loudness: pervasive but diffuse — filed under "UI/controls confusing" rather than named.

**8. No pipette.** Concentrated in Techtonica's first three months. "Sample-Building key" (31 Aug 2023): "AKA 'pipette tool', AKA 'Q key in Factorio'. It's muscle memory for a lot of factory gamers"; dev JoeyFHG: "already a spec for us, it's on the list!" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/0/3815166265398988450/)). "The game has issues." (10 Oct 2023): "Pipette tool, please. Please! PLEASE!! PLEEEEEAAAASE!!!!"; dev: "putting out the second of two QOL patches later this month" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/0/3874842509596336503/)). "this vs satisfactory": Satisfactory has "pipettes, blueprints, item sinks" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/0/4030223299337941984/)). Eight threads total ([search](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/search/?q=pipette&gidforum=0&include_deleted=0)). Not seen in Steam *reviews* — forum-only.

**9. No alt-mode/overlay.** Satisfactory 233-h negative review (Jul 2021): "No signs to label things. Techtonica: "information needed to build things being frequently obscured" ([HardcoreGamer](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-techtonica/)). FOUNDRY suggestions want an "E-key toggle like Satisfactory" for variants ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/983870/discussions/0/4343240481095229278/)). **Thin** — two direct mentions.

**10. No recipe browser.** Techtonica: "Recipes tab in its menu, it's blank with no data" ([HardcoreGamer](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-techtonica/)); a pipette request adds "preferably one that also includes the set recipe" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/0/3802778195619867862/)). **11. Controller / Steam Deck.** Satisfactory 1.0 on Deck: "really cumbersome", some functions needed the touchscreen; proper controller support arrived in 1.1 (June 2025) ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/526870/discussions/0/595145802451520440/), [Yahoo/PC Gamer](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/satisfactorys-biggest-patch-since-launch-110000583.html)). shapez 2 has no controller support and is 97% positive; the 1.0 FAQ just says "some tweaks to the controls" on Deck ([FAQ](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2162800/discussions/0/806849231160779528/)). FOUNDRY: 9 Deck/launch mentions ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/983870/foundry)). ## (i) Five absences most often called "unfinished" / dealbreaker on day one

1. **Blueprints / copy-paste (or gating them).** The only QoL absence with a *counted* review signal (FOUNDRY, 18 mentions, "considered essential"), present in top negative reviews of Satisfactory, DSP, Factory Town, ShapeHero, and in press for Techtonica. It is also the thing modders add first, which reviewers hold against the studio.
2. **Mid-game slog / no goal after first automation.** Not an affordance, but it is the most frequent reason a 30–50-hour player flips to negative (FOUNDRY, Desynced, Techtonica, CoI 35 mentions, Astro Colony, shapez 2). Any audit of "unfinished on day one" must include it.
3. **Accidental deletion with no confirmation and no undo.** Treated as one complaint by players: DSP's 65-reply and 92-reply threads in launch month, CoI's eight undo threads, Satisfactory's "no undo after dismantling 50 things". Satisfactory's dismantle refund and timer are the community's cited reason it is forgiven there.
4. **Performance/optimisation at launch.** Where it fails it dominates (Techtonica 21+13 mentions, Astro Colony 25) and co-occurs with "released an unfinished, unpolished and unoptimized game". It is binary: unremarked when fine, headline when not.
5. **Tutorial/onboarding that does not teach the core loop.** Top complaint for Astro Colony (34) and CoI (44 "learning curve"), first negative review for Desynced, and the "forced/tedious tutorial" line on FOUNDRY. Hidden-QoL (item 7) is the same failure seen from the other side.

Runners-up: belt-drag (2), named by press on day one but rarely leading a review; statistics (4).

## (ii) Five absences reviewers forgive or don't mention

1. **Multiplayer.** DSP's launch complaint was shouted down as entitlement; shapez 2 refuses it at 97% positive; CoI ships without it at Very Positive. Only broken co-op is punished.
2. **Mod support.** Promised "during EA" (FOUNDRY) or delivered at 1.0 (shapez 2) with no detectable review damage.
3. **Controller / Steam Deck.** Satisfactory went six years to 1.1; shapez 2 tells Deck users to use the trackpad. Complaints exist but are "would be nice", except when the store page over-promises (Astro Colony).
4. **Pipette.** Requested loudly by Factorio veterans on Techtonica's forum (eight threads, "muscle memory"), but absent from any Steam review or press piece examined; devs answered "on the list" and the patches followed with no review event.
5. **Localisation quality.** DSP's "just enough off enough to be annoying" English and ShapeHero's machine translation appear in single reviews; neither dented the score.

Alt-overlay (9), recipe browser (10) and cursor-clear (6) sit here by default: evidence is **thin** or **not found** — nobody reviews a factory game down for them.

## Where evidence is thin

Reddit was inaccessible, so "top Reddit threads" loudness is unmeasured throughout. Items 6, 9, 10 have zero to two direct citations. No RPS/Eurogamer text was retrievable. 2025–26 releases covered: ShapeHero Factory, Star Birds (only generic reception found — [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Birds)), Final Factory, shapez 2 1.0 (Apr 2026). Undo figures are forum-thread counts, not review counts.
