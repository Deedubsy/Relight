# Relight standards audit — extra categories (A: avatar body, B: threat readouts, C: city wayfinding)

Researched 2026-09-04 with web search + page fetches. Every claim carries a URL; "not found" means no citable source was reachable (several wikis — Riftbreaker/They-Are-Billions Fandom, Mindustry Miraheze, Hooded Horse, RPS, IGN — blocked fetches, so some rows lean on Steam pages and forum threads).

## A — The avatar's body

### Factorio
- **Inventory grammar**: 80-slot grid (+10 toolbelt research, +10/20/30 for armours) ([Player](https://wiki.factorio.com/Player)). Left-click stack, right-click half, Shift-click "stack transfer", Ctrl-click "inventory transfer: all of the selected stack type" ([Controls](https://wiki.factorio.com/Controls), [fandom Controls](https://factorio-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Controls)). Shift-click from inventory routes buildables to quickbar, guns/ammo/armour to their slots, intermediates to **logistic trash slots** ([forum](https://forums.factorio.com/viewtopic.php?p=103512)).
- **Hand/cursor**: **Q** "returns the item in the cursor stack to the player's inventory" (and is the pipette when empty); **Z** "drop one item of what you are holding on the ground, on a belt, or into a machine"; **F** picks up ground items ([Controls](https://wiki.factorio.com/Controls)).
- **Reach**: "Player reach was increased to 10 tiles" (0.17) ([Player](https://wiki.factorio.com/Player)). Out-of-range display: ghost "appears red, you cannot place it there… green, it can be placed" ([Quick start](https://wiki.factorio.com/Tutorial:Quick_start_guide)); the specific "cannot reach" cursor state: not found on the wiki.
- **Death**: corpse "contains all items… inventory, quickbar, trash slots, and equipment slots"; respawn "10 seconds after being killed… at the center of the world or at a pre-determined spawn point"; corpse no longer despawns since 2.0.7 ([Player](https://wiki.factorio.com/Player)).
- **Health**: 250 HP, "naturally, slowly regenerate a few seconds after taking damage, at 6 hit points per second"; +50/level research ([Player](https://wiki.factorio.com/Player)). No sprint/stamina/dodge; speed 8.9 tiles/s, exoskeleton toggle Alt+E ([Player](https://wiki.factorio.com/Player), [Controls](https://wiki.factorio.com/Controls)).
- **Aiming**: Space "shoots at the nearest enemy to the cursor", C cycles weapon ([Controls](https://wiki.factorio.com/Controls)); damage numbers/hit markers: not found (vanilla has none documented).
- **Light**: "flashlight that automatically turns on during the night time (which transfers to headlights if the player is in a vehicle)" ([Player](https://wiki.factorio.com/Player)).
- **Vehicles as reach**: character/spidertron chart 5×5 chunks around them ([Radar](https://wiki.factorio.com/Radar)); remote building via map/pins (FFF-400 below).
- **Gun as a tool**: Space auto-aims at "the enemy closest to your mouse cursor" ([Quick start](https://wiki.factorio.com/Tutorial:Quick_start_guide)) — one key, no aim skill; combat pressure comes from pollution-triggered attack groups, not player marksmanship ([Enemies](https://wiki.factorio.com/Enemies)).

### The Riftbreaker
- **Inventory**: "inventory screen (default hotkey: I)… each arm able to be equipped with up to three (3) weapons which can be cycled in-game" ([fandom How-to-play](https://riftbreaker.fandom.com/wiki/How_to_play_guide_for_The_Riftbreaker)). Grid/stack/trash grammar: not found.
- **Hand item / reach**: not found (buildings are placed from a build menu; no range figure located).
- **Death**: "Building a headquarters will allow Mr Riggs to respawn… If you die while there is no HQ on the map it is 'game over'" ([fandom](https://riftbreaker.fandom.com/wiki/How_to_play_guide_for_The_Riftbreaker)); "Players can respawn at portals they've constructed, though a hardcore mode removes this safety net" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Riftbreaker)).
- **Health/dodge**: shields regenerate via skills ("Generator Overcharge… regenerates your shields"); "Dash skill that leaves a elemental damage trail is a very good thing to have throughout the game" ([Steam tips guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2837001886)).
- **Light**: "Mr. Riggs' spotlight" / "headlight" cast shadows at night (EXOR ray-tracing note, surfaced in search of Steam news; direct post URL not retrieved).
- **Balance in reviews**: "twin stick stylee… the base building phase is pretty engrossing" ([TheXboxHub](https://www.thexboxhub.com/the-riftbreaker-review/)); "planning out walls and defenses is always in conflict with but complementing exploring" ([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/riftbreaker-game-review/)); "Exploration = incessant combat… balance, and pacing often feel off" ([gameblur](https://gameblur.net/reviews/game-reviews/the-riftbreaker-pc-and-xbox-series-s-review/)); "The waves only get harder as you continue to level up your headquarters… never forces you to upgrade until you're ready" ([WayTooManyGames](https://waytoomany.games/2021/11/16/review-the-riftbreaker/)). Design lever: waves scale with HQ tier, so the factory sets the combat difficulty, and "Waves will attack at the location you are at" ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/780310/discussions/0/3115920924809547113/)).

### Techtonica
- **Inventory**: slot-based encumbrance, "increased from 56 to 80 slots (without upgrades)"; players complain of no visible encumbrance meter ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/0/4286935937412377801/)).
- **Drop/clear**: no drop or destroy — dev: "this is a problem we'd like to solve further down the line in EA"; workaround "respawn has zero penalty and lets you teleport to your closest terminal to dump stuff" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/0/4030223299336715341/), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/discussions/0/3812910660671832718/)).
- **Reach / out-of-range**: not found.
- **Death**: no death; Respawn button teleports to nearest terminal, "Popping out of the world now respawns the player" ([1.0 notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/allnews/)).
- **Light**: "You can now toggle the Groundbreaker's headlamp. Find the option in the settings menu"; "Omniseeker now highlights distance and direction to tracked objects" ([1.0 notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1457320/allnews/)).
- **Combat**: none ([store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1457320/Techtonica/)).

### Satisfactory
- **Inventory**: 18→78 slots, up to six hand slots, "a button to sort all items", Ctrl-click "all items of the same type", trash slot ("Uranium Waste" excepted) ([Inventory](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Inventory)).
- **Reach**: Build Gun "construction and dismantle range is 100 meters (12.5 foundation lengths)"; hologram "hard clearance appears red, and soft clearance appears yellow"; the gun "does not occupy any inventory slot" ([Build Gun](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Build_Gun)). Interaction range 3.5 m (20 m with Hover Pack) ([Pioneer](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Pioneer)).
- **Death**: "camera switches to 3rd person… rag-doll"; respawn at HUB with 30 HP; death crate "contain all items from the pioneer's inventory as well items from equipment slots" ([Health](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Health), [Crate](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Crate)).
- **Health**: "100 health points divided into 10 segments"; below 30 "flashing red"; regen 1.25 HP/s to 30 after 5 s; HUD text "DAMAGE TO FICSIT PROPERTY DETECTED" ([Health](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Health), [Pioneer](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Pioneer)).
- **Sprint**: Shift sprint, no stamina; flashlight; vehicles switch to third person ([Pioneer](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Pioneer)).

**Converged on**: a grid inventory with Shift = stack / Ctrl = all-of-type; a named hand slot with an explicit clear/drop key; a death that leaves a lootable container and respawns at a set point; a numeric/segmented health bar that self-regenerates; an automatic personal light at night; red-vs-green placement ghosts.
**Only one game does**: Satisfactory prints a metric range (100 m) and colour-codes hard/soft clearance; Techtonica removes death entirely and uses "Respawn" as a free teleport; Factorio's corpse now never despawns; Riftbreaker ties wave difficulty to HQ tier so building sets combat pace.

## B — Threat readouts in defence-as-demand games

### Mindustry
- Wave countdown: "The countdown for the next wave starts after the beginning of the previous one… or when the last enemy dies"; first wave has "twice the usual duration" ([Editor/Rules](https://mindustry-unofficial.fandom.com/wiki/Editor/Rules)); wave start "signaled by the countdown of the wave timer and a brief noise" ([Enemy Units](https://mindustry-fan-powered.fandom.com/wiki/Enemy_Units)).
- Composition preview / spawn markers / "core under attack" / range overlay / no-ammo icon: not found in reachable sources (wikis blocked). Store confirms the loop: "feed ammo into your turrets… Defend your sectors from periodic invasions… enemy flier raids" ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1127400/Mindustry/)); turret "Range indicates the distance at which turrets can detect and fire" ([Category:Turret](https://mindustry-unofficial.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Turret)).

### Factorio (incl. Space Age)
- **Alerts**: 16 types incl. `entity_under_attack`, `entity_destroyed`, `turret_out_of_ammo`, `turret_fire`; "small triangle icons on the right of the quickbar"; "Hovering over an alert… shows arrows on the screen edges that point to the positions of the entities"; click to jump ([Alerts](https://wiki.factorio.com/Alerts)). 2.0: alerts overview "separated by planet, and clicking an item will take you to the location on the map"; alerts can be pinned ([FFF-400](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-400)).
- **Threat overlay**: pollution "visible on the map… as a blocky red cloud"; "only shown on the minimap with alt mode on"; Gleba "Spores are displayed as green squares on the map"; production GUI has a pollution tab ([Pollution](https://wiki.factorio.com/pollution)).
- **Scaling readout**: evolution only via console `/evolution` with "percentages for the three sources" (time, pollution, nests destroyed) ([Enemies](https://wiki.factorio.com/Enemies)) — no HUD readout.
- **Vulcanus**: "each individual demolisher patrols a defined territory, and building structures in their territory will disturb them" — territory drawn on the map ([Demolisher](https://wiki.factorio.com/Demolisher)). **Gleba**: "pollution mechanic is based on aroma and spores" that attract pentapods ([FFF-424](https://factorio.com/blog/post/fff-424)).
- **Turret status**: "Warning for ammo-less turret is shown", "Contents of the turret's inventory visible in sidebar" ([Gun turret](https://wiki.factorio.com/Gun_turret)). Countdown/composition preview: none (attacks every "1 to 10 minutes (random)" once spawners absorb pollution) ([Enemies](https://wiki.factorio.com/Enemies)). Post-attack report: destroyed-entity alerts only.

### The Riftbreaker
- Store: "the world will start seeing you as a threat… increasingly difficult waves" ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/780310/The_Riftbreaker/)). "Only the map you are currently in can get attacks"; "Waves will attack at the location you are at"; mining outposts get "a very strong attack" on objective completion ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/780310/discussions/0/3115920924809547113/), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/780310/discussions/0/3467226415502267288/)). Weather hazards "give you a warning on where things are going to hit the ground" ([tips guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2837001886)). Off-map outpost attacks: dev "not final at all… only an idea" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/780310/discussions/0/4137186326783481146/)). Wave timer/threat-map UI specifics: not found.

### They Are Billions
- Store: "Make noise and they will come… hundred of them will come to investigate"; survival "must survive for a specific period of time" ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/644930/They_Are_Billions/)). Final wave text "Infected come from all directions! THEY ARE BILLIONS!!" ([fandom Swarms](https://they-are-billions.fandom.com/wiki/Swarms)). Alerts = sound + "mini-map ping", deliberately suppressed on-screen ("The game wont give warning for things that are on the edge of the screen"); opt-in "show visible alerts" that "will spam you during waves" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/644930/discussions/0/1642042464749067572/)); players ask for thresholds (75/50/25 %) and auto-pause on "Infected colony" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/644930/discussions/0/3277925755431964558/)). Noise *map*: not found (noise is a rule, not an overlay).

### Diplomacy is Not an Option
- "Waves of enemies will arrive on a specific Day"; "see the direction of the first enemy attack on the corner of your mini-map" ([NoobFeed](https://www.noobfeed.com/articles/diplomacy-is-not-an-option-beginners-guide)). Complaint: despite the arrow "they walk by just out of range of my towers"; tip: "Move your camera to where the unit will spawn, then pan it toward your castle" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1272320/discussions/0/599658601187034003/)).
- Dome Keeper: "Use the time between attacks to dig… return to base and prepare for imminent attack"; threats "across air and ground" ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1637320/Dome_Keeper/)).

**Converged on**: an "under attack" alert with a sound, a minimap ping and a click/arrow to the spot; a per-turret "no ammo" state; a per-wave timer where waves are scheduled (Mindustry, DINAO, Dome Keeper) or a pressure overlay where they are emergent (Factorio pollution/spores); alert-fatigue complaints when every wall hit pings.
**Only one game does**: Factorio draws hover-arrows at the screen edge and lists alerts per planet; Vulcanus paints enemy *territory* on the map; DINAO gives a direction arrow days ahead; They Are Billions suppresses on-screen alerts by design (and players hate it).

## C — City setting: navigation and "where am I"

- **Frostpunk 2**: six district types; the Generator is the landmark ("houses the Council Hall and the Generator") ([game-vault](https://frostpunk-2.game-vault.net/wiki/Districts), [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/frostpunk-2-all-districts-list-explained/)); maps differ by terrain ("a dreadnought stretches across a chasm", "Fuel and Materials will always be on two opposite ends") ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/frostpunk-2-apocalyptic-whiteout-every-map-ranked/)). District naming/labels: not found.
- **Against the Storm**: "Dangerous Glades have a skull above them" ([Hooded Horse wiki](https://wiki.hoodedhorse.com/Against_the_Storm/Glades)); glade-reveal perks give only "a text list of names… 'Hidden Trader Cemetery'" without positions; glades stay hidden under forest until opened ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1336490/discussions/0/4624727515590558682/)).
- **Timberborn**: "Editing the name of District Centers sets the name for that District"; selected centre shows a path line that "changes color from green to red" after 70 tiles ([District Center](https://timberborn.wiki.gg/wiki/District_Center), [Districts](https://timberborn.wiki.gg/wiki/Districts)).
- **Cities: Skylines 2**: districts "named automatically but you are able to rename it" ([Paradox wiki](https://cs2.paradoxwikis.com/Districts)); streets auto-named but one name can encircle a city — "there's nothing I can do to fix it"; players install AddressesCS2 ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/949230/discussions/0/4031347532805279018/)).
- **Anno 1800**: "561 island names… Only the player's islands can be edited" ([fandom](https://anno1800.fandom.com/wiki/Island_Names)); monuments built in stages, one per island ([fandom](https://anno1800.fandom.com/wiki/Monuments)); towns "picturesque from a zoomed-out perspective, and full of detail… when zoomed in" ([NGN review](https://www.newgamenetwork.com/article/2104/anno-1800-review/)).
- **Satisfactory**: fog reveals by walking or Radar Towers; compass with player/marker icons; markers have "customizable name, size, icon, color, and Compass view distance"; Stamps via Alt+.; hovering a marker "triggers a Laser Beacon in the World" ([Map](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Map)); craftable Beacons deprecated because "placing markers directly is free" ([Beacon](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Beacon)).
- **Factorio**: chart tags searchable; pins via Alt+RMB with "distance indicators when off-screen", editable captions — motivated by "Halfway there, I kind of lose my place" ([FFF-400](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-400)); fog = "last known state of the chunk… a dim image that cannot be zoomed into" ([Radar](https://wiki.factorio.com/Radar)); Shift-click places a chat tag to a map location ([Controls](https://wiki.factorio.com/Controls)).
- **Dyson Sphere Program**: minimap bottom-left, click for planet map ([gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/dyson-sphere-program/exploration/z7e200)); "no compass… no relationship between the orientation of planet view and that of the main view"; players build "distinctive structures… to act as landmarks", use the sector grid, colour foundations ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/discussions/0/3147430975636383800/)); surface pins were "the top 1 requested feature on this forum" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/discussions/0/3111392579694380906/)).
- **"I got lost" evidence**: DSP "Its easy to get lost on a big planet" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/discussions/0/3147430975636383800/)); Factorio devs' own "lose my place" ([FFF-400](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-400)); CS2 street naming "clunky" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/949230/discussions/0/4031347532805279018/)). Review lines saying "everything looks identical" for FP2/CS2: not found (review sites blocked).

**Converged on**: a full map plus a minimap with the player marked; fog that keeps a stale "last seen" picture; free player pins with an off-screen distance/arrow; auto-generated names that the player can overwrite (CS2 districts, Timberborn districts, Anno islands); one un-missable central landmark (Generator, Hearth, HUB, HQ).
**Only one game does**: Satisfactory's hover-to-laser-beacon and compass-distance tiers; Factorio's map search that finds items by production; Timberborn's green-to-red walk-distance line; Against the Storm's skull as a *threat* marker on the wayfinding map; DSP shipping with no compass and a community that built its own landmarks.
