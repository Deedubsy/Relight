## Recommendation

Make this phase a useful discovery expansion built around **Electricians, the Concrete crew, a riverside Turbine hall, Lamplighters and Surveyors**, alongside the existing station, radio, workshop and repair schematic. Deliver each benefit in the same increment as its discovery. Use explicit local interaction for campaign survivor recruitment, permanent saved unlocks, and physical materials/power for the resulting machines. Do not make recruitment require taking an arbitrary legacy Held block.

This is a proposed revised catalogue, not an assertion that the owner approved these rewards. It deliberately leaves the endgame economy to Q07 and construction automation to Phase 8. Reversible costs, throughput, footprints and placement distances must be documented with each implementation and measured; old catalogue values are not adopted balance targets.

## Implementation boundaries and acceptance

The recommended increments below become executable only after Q09 is settled; they describe acceptance, not a second task-status list.

**P7-01 — saved discoveries and Electricians.** Add a campaign site/recruit catalogue with stable identities, seeded accessible placement, local clues and ordinary in-reach recruitment. Preserve existing saves, the workshop reward/guardian and earned electrical unlocks. Distinguish seeing, recruiting/restoring and operational power state. Guarantee reachable essential capability without requiring personal combat or a circular unlock. Test partial progress, repeated interaction, disabled bases, load/replay and older-save migration.

**P7-02 — Concrete crew and working construction chain.** Discover/recruit the crew, build a paid Mixer and supply real stone/power, output transferable concrete, then build and repair Barricades with conserved inventories. Include machine inspection, construction history/routing integration and clear defensive differences. Prices/HP/rates are provisional implementation proposals, not locked legacy numbers.

**P7-03 — useful facilities and optional exploration rewards.** Restore a reachable riverside Turbine hall with delivered materials and real electrical output; recruit optional Lamplighters/Surveyors with the working capabilities above. Ensure detours do not obstruct station corridors, existing essential installations or guardian clearance. Facility operation must reflect connection/disable state; restoration must not register a new assault base merely because it is a facility. Demonstrate useful supplied-network operation and multiple possible optional discovery orders.

**P7-04 — discovery and recipe information.** Show known sites and earned capabilities, restoration needs, actual service state and what makes/uses each implemented item. Reveal clues through exploration; no omniscient discovery checklist or hidden attack data. Replace obsolete survivor/blueprint statements with honest current capabilities and later availability. Verify ordinary UI interaction, save/load and readable 1366/900-width layouts; retain normal light/power responsiveness measurements.

**P7-05 — integration and engineering review.** Exercise paid acquisition and use of every adopted reward, conservation, alternate orders, seed/route reachability, old/new saves, replay and live defence coexistence. Use seeds 3/4/5 plus holdouts 8/11/13 and a declared broader generation sweep. Run required full code/profile checks and review all adopted P7 acceptance. Record provisional balance/performance findings; automated success does not supply a human phase verdict.

**Shared preparation and play.** New EX-08C freezes the resulting build/starts and prepares a new guide and blank record after P7-05. Keep EX-08B and port 5177 as the earlier Phase 6 checkpoint. Shared EX-08H/P6-H/P7-H and remaining T18 observations follow EX-08C; include the original ammo retest and credit the owner's earlier factory/transport result. Human tasks are deferred, not waived or passed. Wider EX-09/Q07 remains dependent on the eventual representative-loop assessment.
