# Map-view prototype — test plan (Gate A)

The prototype is `packages/game` (`packages/proto` until Phase 4; `npm run dev -w @relight/game`, then `http://127.0.0.1:5173/?seed=3`). It tests one question from §25: is choosing blocks on a map with a front count interesting on its own? Everything that would answer it better with tiles, belts, or lighting is in `DEFERRED.md`. This is the Phase 2 gate protocol of the programme; the human who runs it writes `TEST_RESULTS.md` and its `verdict:` line.

## Protocol

- **Five testers**, one at a time, same seed, two scenarios each, A then B, with a break between. Economy on, scattered map on. No `autoplay`. The HUD export's `meta.configHash` must read **`ee23bb1c`**, the calibrated config (`packages/sim/src/types.ts` `PROTO_CALIBRATED`, `docs/experiments/calibration.md`); an export with another hash is a different game and does not count.
- **Scenario A — fresh start.** `?seed=3` (the "Copy link" button copies it). Two and a half hours of sim time at 4× (key `2`; the sim clock is in the HUD; the observer ends the session when it passes 2:30:00). About 38 minutes of real time. Tests shape and enclose-to-build.
- **Scenario B — mid-game snapshot.** `?state=b-compact-seed3` loads the compact bot's position at 3:00:00 on seed 3 (28 held, 11 front, 18 interior, 3 assemblers, 0 lost; `packages/game/public/snapshots/b-compact-seed3.json`, regenerated by `npm run snapshot` and checked in CI by `npm run snapshot:check`). The page opens **paused** with a toast; the tester presses space or `2`. Ninety minutes of sim time at 4×, ending when the clock passes 4:30:00. About 23 minutes of real time. Tests pressure, ring order and retreat: the snapshot's start patch is gone, four blocks are dug out, and steel runs out about forty minutes in (expected timeline below).
- **One control session**, a sixth tester, Scenario A only, with `?seed=3&economy=0` (hash **`825d2d09`**): the same map with claims and assemblers free and no rubble; machine slots still apply. It does not count toward the five; it is there to separate what the front rule does from what the steel budget does.
- **No instructions beyond §5's rules**, read aloud once before Scenario A starts, verbatim from `RELIGHT-design.md`: the paragraphs *Block states*, *Rot*, *Waking*, *How a block becomes Held* and *Frontage*, nothing after the cost table. Before Scenario B the observer says only: "This is a saved game three hours in. Same rules." The panel's own hint text says the same thing in three sentences; nothing else is explained. Questions during the session get "whatever you think" as the answer.
- **The observer** sits behind the tester with a notepad and writes down, with the sim clock, every unprompted remark about:
  - the **shape** of the territory ("I want to close that gap", "I'm going to go around the well", "this is getting long");
  - the **ring order** (the drag list on the right) and the **pips** — a pip is a glyph, not just a colour: ● green, ▲ amber, ✕ red (blinking);
  - the **machine slots** (the small square in a held block: outline = free interior slot, filled = machine, red = machine on a block that is back on the front) and the greyed build button with its reason;
  - **rubble running out** (the strip at the top of a held block fades; the start block is dug out at about 2:00);
  - the **skyline and survivors** — facility silhouettes appear on the map only within 6 blocks of a held block; a survivor marker appears when a neighbouring block is held and toasts `Electricians: "We're in."` when its own block is held. Any remark about heading for a silhouette or a survivor counts as a shape remark.
  Prompted remarks (answers to a question) are logged separately and do not count.
- At the end of each scenario the tester presses **Export telemetry JSON**; the file is named `relight-seed3-<simclock>.json` and carries `meta.scenario` (`A` or `B`) and `meta.snapshot`. The observer collects the files and the notes before the post-test questions. **Save snapshot** (next to it) writes the raw sim state and is for bug reports, not for the results.

## Expected timeline — Scenario A

From the calibration harness (`npm run calibrate -- --md docs/experiments/calibration.md`, config `ee23bb1c`, seeds 3/4/5, 3 h; event traces on seed 3). Bots claim every 15 min in hour one and every 5 min after, so a tester at that pace holds about 4 blocks at 1:00 and 16 at 2:00. Times are sim clock; ranges are across the three seeds.

| Sim clock | Compact play (blob around the start) | Straight push north (spike) | Quiet-block play (cheapest) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:31 | 200 start rounds fill two of the HQ's three hoppers; the third shows ▲ then ✕ for up to 30 s while the Mk1 assembler fills it; every pip ● from 0:31 (the C10 transient, `PHASE_2_REPORT.md`) | same | same |
| 0:31–1:00 | 1 assembler (the HQ's Mk1), 10 mag/min; magazine stock climbs to the 400 cap by 1:15; no free slot, so the build button is greyed with the reason; the Skyline panel shows the **Arsenal** only (6 blocks from the HQ; the smoke test confirmed it at t = 0) — the Foundry (10 away) appears when a claim brings it within 6 | same; every block the push holds is front, so it never has a slot | same |
| 1:00 | first enclosure on the 4th claim (all seeds): one interior slot appears | no interior, ever, on any seed | first enclosure 1:00 |
| 1:03–1:11 | a HUD-watcher buys the second assembler from start stock the minute the slot exists (1:03, 1:03, 1:11); **Electricians** join at ~1:05 ("We're in.") | **▲ and ✕ at 1:05, first block lost at 1:08**, then the same 2 blocks fall and are retaken every 5 min (18 falls by 2:30); nothing can be built because there is no slot; stock is 0 from 1:15 | second assembler 1:03–1:08; Electricians ~1:15 |
| 1:15–2:00 | every pip ● whatever the tester does; stock sits at 400; no amber in the session (this is open decision D-P2-2, edges per assembler) | ✕ on the far edge every bloom | as compact |
| 2:00 | start patch empty and the HQ block dug out (its rubble strip gone; `run-dry` event); the next start-row blocks empty at 2:15, 2:30, 2:45; steel then comes only from held industrial blocks; 2 slots used | 1 used / 0 free; steel never short because it never grows | as compact; a third assembler at 1:47–2:11 on two seeds |
| 2:15–2:20 | at bot pace the Arsenal is reached 2:15 and the **Concrete crew** join 2:20 (seed 3) | — | Concrete crew ~1:45 |
| 2:30 | session ends: held ≈ 22 at bot pace, front 10–17, 0 lost, 2 assemblers, stock 400 | ≈ 4 held, 9 front, 18 falls on 2 blocks | ≈ 22 held, 2–3 assemblers, 0 lost |

What the table says the test can and cannot see: the front's price shows as **shape**, not as a pip. A push holds four blocks and can never build because none of them is interior; a blob has its first slot at 1:00 and never sees a pip in the session whether or not it builds — one Mk1 at 10 mag/min feeds a 16-block blob's front, so the second assembler is bought for nothing the tester can see. Whether a tester reads the slot square, the fading rubble strip, the skyline and the survivors as reasons to enclose or to head somewhere is what the observer notes are for. The harness could not put compact's first amber inside the session with the levers the constitution allows (start production, start rounds, pool, yield, costs — `PHASE_2_REPORT.md` "Measured"); the constitution says that is the edges-per-assembler constant showing itself, and it is the first open decision for this gate, not something to tune around.

Under `economy=0` (hash `825d2d09`) assemblers are free but still need an interior slot, so the "no slot" rows hold for every shape until the tester encloses; rubble does not run out; there is no 2:00 patch event.

## Expected timeline — Scenario B

From the snapshot run forward with the compact bot continuing (claim every 5 min, build when demand > 80 % of production and a slot is free) and, separately, with nobody acting. Seed 3 only; the snapshot is one state, not three.

| Sim clock | If the tester keeps expanding like the bot | If the tester does nothing |
|---|---|---|
| 3:00 (start) | paused; 28 held / 11 front / 18 interior; steel 1,799, copper 16,736, magazine stock 400; production 50 mag/min against demand ≈ 24; 4 blocks dug out; every pip ● | same |
| 3:00–3:40 | claims continue; steel falls ~45/min (magazines cost 2 steel each and only held industrial blocks yield it); **steel 0 at ≈ 3:40** | steel 0 at ≈ 3:50 |
| 3:40–3:50 | assemblers stop; stock drains from 400; **▲ ×3 and ✕ ×3 by 3:50** | stock drains more slowly |
| 4:00 | first blocks fall: 29 held, 6 lost | stock still draining |
| 4:10–4:20 | cascade along the ring: 18 held / 17 lost at 4:10, 11 / 24 at 4:20; assemblers fall with their blocks | stock 0 by 4:20; first loss ≈ 4:20 |
| 4:30 (end) | 2 held, 33 lost — the bot never reorders and never retreats | 22 held, 6 lost |

So B is the steel wall: the tester either claims industrial blocks north (steel income), reorders the ring so the edges they care about starve last, or lets the outer blocks go. Nothing in the panel says which. "Claim-to-enclose visible" (`TEST_RESULTS.md` §4) is a claim whose tooltip shows `closes ≥ 1` while any pip is ▲ or ✕.

## Metrics

All from the telemetry export unless marked observer. Every count in `summary` is for the session only — a Scenario B export counts claims and losses after 3:00:00, not the bot's.

| Metric | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Territory shape at 2.5 h (A): bounding-box aspect (w/h), perimeter/area, held, interior | `minutes[]` last row; `summary.shape` | Distinct shapes are the test |
| Claims per hour | `summary.claimsPerHour` | §25 Q10, the §18 cadence question (C2) |
| Blocks lost | `losses[]`, `summary.lost` | Whether the front is felt |
| Ring ever reordered (B above all) | `reorders[]` | Whether the ammo order is a decision |
| Time of first enclosure (A) | `summary.firstEnclosure` | Whether Interior is discovered without being taught |
| Assemblers built, and when against the first amber pip | `assemblers[]`, `summary.firstAmber`, `summary.firstRed` | Whether ammo production is managed, ignored, or managed only once a pip turns |
| Config hash | `meta.configHash` | Must be `ee23bb1c` (`825d2d09` for the control) |
| Scenario and snapshot | `meta.scenario`, `meta.snapshot`, `meta.startT` | Which table an export belongs to |
| Machine slots used / free at 1 h, 2 h, end; assemblers rejected | `minutes[]` (`slotsUsed`, `slotsFree`, `atRisk`), `assemblersRejected[]` | Whether the tester reads the interior as the place the factory goes |
| Blocks dug out (rubble gone) and when | `dry[]`, `summary.ranDry` | Whether finite rubble is noticed before it bites |
| Facilities reached, survivors joined | `held[]` events with `facility` / `survivor` | Whether the skyline pulls |
| Unprompted shape remarks | observer notes | The other half of "interesting" |

**Distinct shapes.** Two testers' Scenario A territories at 2:30 count as distinct if their perimeter/area ratios differ by more than 0.15 or their bounding-box aspects differ by more than 0.3. `GAME-ASSUMPTION`: these thresholds are picked to separate the sim's compact (aspect ≈ 1, ratio ≈ 0.7 at 30 blocks) from its spike (aspect ≈ 0.4, ratio ≈ 1.2); nothing in the doc sets them. Perimeter counts Held sides facing Dark or Contested only, so inert and the map edge do not inflate it.

## Outcomes

- **Success (`go`)** — at least 3 of the 5 Scenario A territories are distinct by the rule above, at least one unprompted shape remark, and at least 2 testers reorder the ring at least once in Scenario B. Phase 3 absorbs the gate: locks the constants §8 of the results informs, redraws §18.
- **Rework** — shapes converge on one policy. Name it by matching each territory to the closest sim bot (compact, spike, balanced, cheapest, river) on aspect and ratio, and say which one won. The tuning surface is the four numbers per district that §24 names (dmax, g, bloom base, bloom slope) plus scatter density. E8 (claim cadence) informs a convergence on compact, because if claims are cheap enough to be constant the front count stops mattering; E7 informs a convergence away from spike, because spike loses blocks on the scattered map; E6 informs a convergence on river or cheapest, because inert-as-solid makes the scattered cells free walls.
- **Kill** — nobody talks about shape and nobody reorders the ring. Go back to §23's rejected list and pick the next mechanic; the front rule as a map-view decision is not carrying the game.

Anything in between (say two distinct shapes, or reorders without remarks) is rework, not success: the bar is the one above, and the sample is five.

## Post-test questions

Asked in this order, open-ended, after both exports. The observer writes the answers down and does not follow up.

1. Tell me about the territory you ended up with.
2. Was there a moment where you changed your mind about where to go next? What happened?
3. What did the list on the right mean to you?
4. What did you want to do that you could not?
5. If you played the same map again tomorrow, what would you do differently?
