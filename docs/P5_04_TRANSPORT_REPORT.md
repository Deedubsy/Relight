# P5-04 — physical truck and selected-stop routes

2026-09-07. Implemented and verified under “Ok move onto P5-04”. Q08 places the truck at the restored second-area station. Existing work and evidence remain uncommitted; PROGRESS owns status.

## Contracts recorded before implementation

One campaign truck appears on the nearest available street parking footprint near the restored station; if obstructed, availability waits for a clear parking space rather than deleting a machine. Existing restored previews receive it on their next simulation tick. It is not a carried kit item. Provisional inherited defaults: 2×3 tiles north/south, 3×2 east/west; 200 cargo stacks; three-times-walk speed; no fuel chain. Cargo stays in the vehicle and pockets retain their ordinary 40-stack capacity. Transfers use ordinary commands and engineer reach. Boarding requires a nearby position within 1.5 tiles of the vehicle; exit chooses a clear adjacent tile or refuses. These are technical implementation defaults, not new owner-approved balance.

WASD drives with the complete street/machine footprint checked through substeps. Turning checks the rotated footprint before movement. Belts/track and existing noncolliding trams can be crossed; other standing machines obstruct the vehicle. Parked trucks obstruct walking and construction. Sprint/dodge and map auto-walk do not drive the truck; map clicks explain manual driving. Normal seated engineer reach is retained. Knockdown parks the vehicle with cargo intact and clears boarding; normal engineer recovery returns the player home. No vehicle damage/combat or autonomous Line truck system is introduced.

Selected-stop map lines are read-only views of actual track and stop/vehicle state, including severed routes, unsupported branches, unpowered/full stops and reserved cargo interruption. Selection is renderer state, not a gameplay command or new routing rule. Map clicks can select a stop; stop inspection and freight panels can select it too. Deselecting hides the route. Existing multi-stop transfers, requests/reserves and return freight remain unchanged.

## Implementation and compatibility

[truck.ts](../packages/sim/src/truck.ts) owns parking, rectangular collision, local boarding, exit, cargo and validation; [transport.ts](../packages/sim/src/transport.ts) queries the existing tram traversal and freight reservations. [construction.ts](../packages/sim/src/construction.ts) dispatches the two ordinary vehicle operations. Walking, placement and the conservation ledger recognise the vehicle. Cargo contains the existing inventory item types, including carried machines and fractional ammunition; it is not a second copy of pockets. Oversized transfers fill available partial-stack room without exceeding either inventory limit. Walking paths and block-travel fallback cannot move a seated truck.

Parking searches within 24 tiles of the restored station, avoids the engineer and the surveyed starter track/platforms, and retries when placement or engineer position changes. The truck persists independently of station power. Legacy truck toggling and benchmark state remain unchanged. Optional campaign truck metadata does not rewrite old saves at load time. Old restored previews receive it on the next tick; if an old preview had its capacity-toggle truck active, that inventory moves intact into the new parked vehicle. Such pre-vehicle restored-preview logs are marked incomplete for replay against the new factory. Ordinary new saves preserve truck position, heading, cargo and occupancy, and retain full-log replay.

World view draws the vehicle footprint, wheels and cab. E boards beside it and exits from the seat; F on the truck or the public Truck cargo button opens load/unload controls. Seated construction uses pocket stock and ordinary reach. Map clicks select a station without moving the engineer; the service summary is brought into view. Selecting it again, Clear route or Escape hides the overlay. Track paths are cyan when connected and amber when service is interrupted, with ready/unpowered/full stop shapes and tram positions. Selection and inspection remain outside save/hash state.
