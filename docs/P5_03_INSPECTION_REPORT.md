## Contracts recorded before implementation

Reuse sim-owned status, physical campaign circuits and the existing item ledger. Inspection is read-only; setting changes continue through ordinary commands and reach checks. F inspects the entity under the pointer without replacing E's established interactions. E also opens inspection for machines that previously only reported a toast. A selected machine is followed by identity, so removal never silently selects its replacement.

Nominal recipe input/output rates describe full-power continuous production; power-limited capacity uses the selected local circuit, while measured output counts actual completed production. Belts, routing devices and inserters report measured transfers separately from production. Contents distinguish held, buffered, input, output, platform and arrival stock. Power numbers describe the selected circuit, not total city supply. Existing brightness rules remain unchanged.

Provisional observation default: keep at most 61 one-second counter samples for a trailing window of up to 60 simulation seconds. Counters and samples are sim-owned, optional and saved in the campaign profile; paused wall time never enters the rate denominator. New/old saves without history start measuring on their next simulation tick; no past machine production is invented. Whole-factory totals reuse recorded extraction/production and all ledger consumption sinks (including construction, fuel and ammunition), explicitly labelled. Recipe changes keep per-item observations rather than relabelling earlier products. Packing/rebuilding starts new machine observations; historical factory totals remain. Legacy state fingerprints are preserved.

## Implemented behaviour

[inspection.ts](../packages/sim/src/inspection.ts) provides machine inspection, circuit queries, factory statistics and validated saved observations. The renderer only reads these queries. Recipe, filter and priority selectors dispatch existing ordinary commands, report refusals and disable configuration out of reach. F permits observation without a reach requirement; E preserves the established chest, freight, repair, discovery and restoration actions. E opens inspection for other machines instead of a transient report toast. Selection follows the machine id, including moving trams; removed machines report removal rather than inspecting a replacement at the same tile.

The panel separates full-power recipe input/output, power-limited capacity and measured completed production. Assemblers count completed output items; excavators count extracted units. Belts, inserters, splitter and underground endpoints count successful transfers out, including inserter extraction from conveyors. These are per-machine observations, never added to whole-factory production. Contents distinguish inputs/inventory, finished output, held items, routing buffers, home stock, station platform/arrivals and tram cargo. Footprints, facing, applicable working ranges/capacities and routing settings are exposed. Generators show their generation capacity and actual load share. Broader freight route visualization remains P5-04.

Circuit information comes from the selected campaign circuit. Poles are queried as physical network nodes rather than assuming their street-owner block is their circuit. Generator status uses its current load share; substation status uses actual campaign supply. Grid cache identity includes disabled cores and workshop restoration, so paused changes do not retain those stale readings. Belt status distinguishes an empty/moving queue from a blocked exit. Inserter pickup and status share the same source/filter/destination query; held-item blockage remains visible even when the source/destination is removed. These reuse the actual sim rules, not a second renderer power model.

The public Item statistics disclosure shows all eight item totals and measured rates without debug mode. Produced means extracted or crafted; Used includes recipe inputs, construction, restoration, repair, coal and fired/lost ammunition under the existing ledger definitions. Transfers do not change either total. Rates use net counter changes: a negative use rate means recipe inputs were returned. The displayed measured duration is the actual available interval, with an em dash before any elapsed simulation time. Sampling trims to a one-second boundary within the last 60 seconds; a partial window is labelled with its exact duration. Save/load retains the window, recipe changes retain per-item history, and observations are validated for finite counters, chronological bounded sample count and non-future ticks. Legacy totals remain readable, but the new saved rate history is campaign-only.

Machine status marks, tooltips and the inspection panel use the same glyph vocabulary. Working devices have in-place marks; blocked belts show their mark and routine belts show it on hover. Hover text refreshes while the pointer stays still, including after a recipe change while paused. No map brightness rule, translation, graph/overlay art completion or human readability approval is introduced.
