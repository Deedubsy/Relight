## Gate B — the extra rows

**The burn-off.** *The question asked was whether the lights coming on landed —
whether it was worth stopping for.* **It was not answered as asked**, and what
came back instead is a request to change what the lights are:

> "I think I want the lighting system to not be tied to the map. The player should
> have to go out and power them themselves. I'd like it to be more like the
> following: Player defends for a while → player mines more, sets up automation,
> builds more generators → player finally has enough power or materials to go to
> the next area to light it up → player builds power poles and lights to go to the
> power box or something of the next area → area gets attacked because it's
> starting to light up, sometimes no attack. At the moment the player character
> doesn't do much of this. I want the player to feel like they're exploring more."

That is **D-GB-1**, and it is a different core loop, not a tune of this one. The
doc's loop is *claim on the map → the block burns off → the lights come on as the
payoff*; the asked-for loop is *earn the power → walk it out yourself → the lights
are the thing you built, and the attack is the answer to it*. **The burn-off's
own row therefore stays empty**: whether the current payoff lands was not
established, and the player's attention was on what the payoff should be instead.

**The first rifle shot, and whether it mattered.** No minute was taken. On whether
it mattered: *"I was just shooting at things."* **No rescue, nothing saved.** This
agrees with the sim at both scales — E-rifle's steady hour is 45 rounds across

has been waiting for. Two asks came with it: *"I'd want the range increased or
have no range"* (**D-GB-3**) and *"we need more enemies when the base is being
attacked. More roaming enemies as well when the player is moving so the player's
weapons mean something"* (**D-GB-4**).

## What this session opened — four rows

Written to `DECISIONS.md` as **D-GB-1 … D-GB-4**. Under **constitution rule 12**
("prompts do not carry constants or rules") none of them has been built; they are
listed, their conflicts named, and the work stops here.

| row | what was asked for | what it conflicts with |
|---|---|---|
| **D-GB-1** | Light earned and walked out by the player, not granted by a claim; an exploration loop with attacks that answer the lighting-up | **§5, §10, §11, M5** — the whole claim → burn-off → payoff chain, and the design's stated best moment |
| **D-GB-2** | "A small quest system" — the game should say what to do next | **Constitution rule 8**: "No tutorial screens. §11 is the tutorial." Also `STANDARDS.md` 4.1, which is in `## Considered` for this exact reason |

| **D-GB-4** | More enemies on base attacks; roaming enemies while walking, "so the player's weapons mean something" | §7's densities, §19's 10 % shooting and 5 % danger caps, and §19's own rule that if shooting exceeds 10 % **"the turret rules are wrong, not the rifle"** |

**The three of them point the same way.** D-GB-1, D-GB-3 and D-GB-4 together ask
for a game in which the player fights, explores and lights the world personally.
The design doc's game is one in which turrets fight, the map is claimed, and the
engineer builds the thing that does the fighting — §19 caps the player's shooting
at a tenth of an hour **on purpose**. Both are coherent games. **They are not the
same game**, and the programme cannot serve both. That choice is D-GB-1's real
content and it is the human's.

## Verdict

gate's four questions.

**What "with conditions" means here.** Gate B passes on one partial hands-on
session, not on a scored hour. Phase 5 opens, and **D-GB-1 to D-GB-4 travel with
it as conditions to be resolved inside it** rather than before it:

- **D-GB-1 is decided, as the hybrid (option c).** The claim still burns off its

  stand — and **powering and lighting the next area becomes a physical expedition
  the engineer walks**, with an area lighting up acting as a threat trigger beside
  the wake bloom. Decided by Daniel, 2026-09-05. **Not built by this gate**; it is
  Phase 5 / 6 work and the doc sentences it moves (§5, §10, §11, §19) move on the
