## Player behaviour

Recruit the Foreman, restore the second-area station to obtain the existing truck, and queue blueprints. **Truck construction** lets you select a physical supply chest and either one waiting order or all currently waiting orders. **Start truck delivery** assigns those identities; later queued orders are not silently added. The truck travels to the chosen chest, loads the required available supplies into its separate real cargo, drives along streets and pays for planned machines locally. Raw materials use ordinary prices; a packed machine replaces its material cost. Local **Put/Take 1 packed machine** controls let players supply physical chests from their pockets, respecting reach and chest/pocket capacity.

The visible phase and reason distinguish loading, travelling, building, waiting, paused and complete. Shortages/full cargo, missing sources, occupied destinations and blocked routes wait and retry after a fix. A vanished source must be explicitly reselected; a new chest at the same tile has a different identity. **Pause truck**, **Resume truck** and **Stop truck job** retain cargo and placed machines. Cancelling an order through the library stops its remaining work; other selected waiting orders can continue. Boarding the truck pauses automatic work before manual driving. Exit does not silently resume it.

The engineer's position and inventory are never exchanged with the truck's. Automatic construction calls the same settings/terrain/unlock/price checks against actual truck cargo and service reach. It leaves existing manual undo history unchanged; real placed machines can still be packed through normal controls. One missing part, or a complete intended underground pair, is built atomically. Matching manually built parts are skipped. No new vehicle, global inventory, fuel system or free disabled-base recovery is introduced.

## Saved data and provisional limits

- Existing 2×3 street footprint, speed ×3 and 200 cargo stacks. Sources are physical chest machines; the home/global supplies and tram commodity storage are not packed-machine stores.
- Loading within 3 tiles of the chest, up to 20 items per second; service within 8 tiles of each planned footprint; one machine/tunnel pair per second. Loading plans a batch for the selected remaining work, bounded by current cargo capacity and available source stock.
- Deterministic A* supports forward/reverse movement and quarter turns. It searches up to 12000 nodes and saves at most 2048 route steps; failed searches wait with feedback. Every actual segment rechecks street, current machine footprint and the engineer's body at substeps no larger than 0.2 tiles. No point-sized path or teleport fallback.
- Waiting retries at 2-second intervals. Pause/takeover clears the route; resume recomputes from the actual parked position and current stock/geometry. Cancelling or manually completing work invalidates the current target before further travel/building.
- Optional `truck.work` schema 1 retains source/order IDs, phase/reason, route/target and next action tick. Metadata 10, recruits 4 and geometry stay unchanged. Older saves keep automatic work absent. Validation rejects malformed control state, identities, timing, oversized paths, non-finite or teleport/sideways route steps and active automation with an occupied truck. Source/order removals remain valid recoverable saved history.

These defaults are provisional agent choices under D-EX-42. They are not balance targets or human usability evidence.
