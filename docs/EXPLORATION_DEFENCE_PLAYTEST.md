# Relight — representative exploration and defence playtest

Version 2 protocol, 2026-09-06. **Not run.** This defines EX-08's human observation; it contains no invented player results. The old Gate A/B protocols and reports retain their historical meaning.

## Prerequisites

EX-04–07 integrated on an identified version/profile, D-EX-Q01–06 settled for the tested slice, and the relevant automated checks recorded. The observer records build, seed, difficulty, day duration, assault/rest settings, warning capability and known failures before play. Define any pass/fail thresholds before running the session; do not tune them after seeing the result.

## Session

Start at the cul-de-sac with the specified initial inventory. The observer does not explain systems unless the player is unable to continue; every intervention is recorded. The player establishes home defence, chooses an expedition, finds the second area, uses tram access, restores the radio opportunity and connects outlying station factories. Include the optional discovery, distinct site encounter, routine raid and a major assault. Continue through recovery and a further expedition so the rest interval and older factory's usefulness are actually observed.

Scheduled and restoration-triggered assaults need coverage. If one ordinary session cannot observe both, use a separately labelled follow-up scenario; do not spawn an event through debug commands and present it as normal progression. Do not compress the day for a pacing judgement without explicitly reporting that changed condition.

## Observations

| Question | Evidence to record |
|---|---|
| Does the opening pay off? | Time and actions before the first practical reward; what the player notices and then uses. |
| Does roaming feel self-directed? | Chosen direction and reason, detours, clues noticed, discoveries missed and need for instructions. |
| Can the player leave a supplied base? | Minor raids resolved by automation, forced returns, ammunition/repair failures and ignored alerts. |
| Is the network understandable? | What each station sends/receives, cargo starvation, time spent correcting settings and the older site's later contribution. |
| Is warning useful? | Information actually available, target interpretation, travel/preparation time and surprises caused by misleading UI. |
| Does defence reward preparation and participation? | Wall/turret layout, supply decisions, personal actions and recoverable failures. |
| Is the rhythm less repetitive? | Time exploring, walking, building, fighting and handling repairs/supplies; repeated chores and voluntary next expedition. |
| Are rewards and losses legible? | Player's account of what restoration changed, why a position failed and how to recover. |

## Result record

Record observed facts, player comments, observer interventions, unobserved questions, and required revisions separately. The owner makes the human gate decision. A successful bot, an implemented feature or a design approval does not fill this result record. Follow-up changes must cite the observation or explicit design decision they address.
